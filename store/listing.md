## English

### Subtitle — App Store (30)
Medicine reminders for family

### Short description — Google Play (80)
A reminder for every dose, and a family that knows you're okay or need help.

### Promotional text — App Store (170)
Your medicine on time, your family by your side: clear reminders for older adults, and relatives who see how they're doing and get help requests with the location.

### Description (4000)
Afiati reminds you of every dose on time, and reassures your children and relatives without worried calls or repeated questions.

Designed for older adults first: large text, clear buttons and few steps. In Arabic and English, with the Hijri date.

Reminders you won't miss
• An on-time notification you can mark the dose from in one tap.
• In the app, the dose card flashes with a chime for 15 minutes until you mark it. You can turn the chime off in Settings.
• Today's doses, grouped by morning, noon, evening and night.
• Marked one by mistake? Undo it in one tap.

Your family with you
• Invite your son or daughter with a 6-digit code or a QR code.
• You approve, and you choose what each relative sees: everything, taken or not only, or missed-dose alerts only.
• If a dose is an hour late, they get an alert and can send you a gentle reminder.
• When a medicine is running low, they know so they can buy it.

The “I need help” button
• One tap sends an urgent alert to everyone who follows you, with your location on the map if you allow it.
• The alert repeats until someone answers. When they do, you hear “… is coming to you” with a calm chime.
• The 997 ambulance and your emergency number are always one tap away.

Your health in one place
• Your medicines with doses, times and stock, with a reminder before you run out.
• An estimate of your pulse with the phone camera, measured on your phone, with no photo saved.
• A log for blood pressure, blood sugar and temperature, and your adherence day by day.

Works offline
Reminders and dose logging are saved on your phone first, and sync with your family when you're back online.

Your privacy, your choice
No password and no ads, and we never sell your data. Your location is sent only when you ask for help, and you can delete your account and all your data from the app at any time.

Note: Afiati helps with reminders and follow-up. It does not diagnose and does not replace your doctor or pharmacist. Pulse readings are estimates. In an emergency, call 997 directly.

## Settings in App Store Connect

- **Primary category:** Medical. **Secondary:** Health & Fitness.
- **Age rating:** answer "None" to every question, which gives 4+. The app has no medical or treatment information, no user-to-user messaging and no web content.
- **Price:** free. No in-app purchases.
- **Copyright:** © 2026 Shaye Alshaye
- **App Review notes** (paste into "Notes"):
  - No login is needed: open the app, tap «لنبدأ / Let's start», type any name and number, then «ابدأ استخدام عافيتي».
  - To see family features, install on a second device. On the first device: Family > Invite a family member, which shows a 6-digit code. On the second device: Family > I follow > Follow a relative by code. Approve on the first device.
  - The pulse measurement uses the back camera and flash on the device only. It is labelled as an estimate, not a diagnosis.
  - The help button alerts approved relatives only. It does not call emergency services by itself; the 997 button opens the phone dialer.
  - Account deletion: Settings > Delete my account and data.

## App Privacy (App Store) — what to declare

Tracking: **No.**

Data collected, all **linked to the user** and **not used for tracking**:

| Type | Data | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Info | Name, Phone Number, Email Address (optional) | App Functionality |
| Health & Fitness | Health (medicines and dose history) | App Functionality |
| Location | Precise Location (only with a help request) | App Functionality |
| Identifiers | Device ID (random app identifier) | App Functionality |
| Usage Data | Product Interaction (days the app was used) | Analytics |
| Location | Coarse Location (country only, from the connection) | Analytics |

Not collected:
- Pulse and vital signs stay on the phone.
- Photos: the camera image is never saved.
- Contacts.
