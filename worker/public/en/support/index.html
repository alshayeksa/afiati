Support

Answers to the most common questions. If you can't find what you need, email us. We usually reply within one business day.

Contact us

How do I add a medicine?

Tap the (+) button at the bottom, type the medicine name, then choose the dose, times and repeat. Add the stock if you'd like a reminder before it runs out.

How do I edit or stop a medicine?

In “My medicines”, tap the medicine, then “Edit medicine” or “Stop”. When stopped, its reminders end and what you logged stays in your history.

I marked a dose by mistake. How do I undo it?

Tap the lit check mark next to the dose, then choose “I didn't take it”. The dose goes back to pending and the stock is restored.

I'm not getting reminders

Open “Settings” and make sure “Medicine notifications” is on. On Android, turn on “Exact-time reminders” and exclude Afiati from battery saving. On iPhone, make sure no Focus mode is silencing it.

How do I turn off the in-app alert sound?

In “Settings”, turn off “Medicine time alert sound”. The dose card still flashes, and the usual notification still arrives.

How can my son or daughter follow me?

In “Family”, tap “Invite a family member” and send the code. Your relative enters it in their app under “Family”, “I follow”, then “Follow a relative by code”. You then get their request to approve and choose what they see.

What happens when I tap “I need help”?

A short countdown starts so you can cancel if you pressed by mistake. Then everyone who follows you gets an urgent alert with your location if you allowed it, repeated every 2 minutes until someone answers. When they do, you see “… is coming to you”. When you're fine, tap “I'm okay now”.

Is the pulse measurement accurate?

It's an estimate, not a medical diagnosis. For the best reading, sit comfortably, cover the back camera and flash gently with your fingertip without pressing, and keep still until it finishes.

How do I change the language or make text larger?

Tap the language button at the top of the home screen, or open “Settings” to choose the language and turn on “Larger text”.

How do I delete my account?

In “Settings”, tap “Delete my account and data”, then “Delete for good”. If you no longer have the app, request deletion on the Delete account page.

I see “The service is paused” or “An update is available”

The first means we paused the service for maintenance. Your medicine reminders still arrive, and the app comes back by itself. The second means your version is out of date, so tap “Update the app”.
